# Social Olympics — User Manual

Social Olympics is a cross-platform web and mobile application for creating and competing in custom Olympic-style competitions with friends, colleagues, or any group.

## 1. Getting Started

### Creating an Account

Visit the Social Olympics web app or download the mobile app and tap **Register**.

You will need to provide:
- A display name
- A valid email address
- A password
- Your date of birth

**Age requirement:** You must be 16 or older to register. Accounts for users under 16 are blocked at registration.

Once registered you will receive a confirmation email. Click the link in the email to activate your account, then log in.

### Logging In

Go to the login page and enter your email and password. If you forget your password, use the **Forgot Password** link to receive a reset email.

### Setting Up Your Profile

After logging in, go to **Profile → Settings** to complete your profile:

- **Profile photo** — upload any photo; it is cropped to a square and resized for you
- **City** — where you are based
- **Country** — select the country you represent
- **Bio** — a short description about yourself
- **Favourite sport** — shown on your public profile

Your display name and avatar appear on leaderboards, the activity feed, and the podium screen.

## 2. Joining a Competition

### Finding Public Competitions

Go to **Discover** from the sidebar or navigation bar. Public competitions are listed here and can be browsed and joined directly.

### Joining via Invite Code

If a host has shared an 8-character invite code with you:

1. Go to **Join with code** from the sidebar or dashboard
2. Enter the invite code
3. Tap **Join Competition**

You will be added as a competitor immediately.

### Joining via Invite Link

Hosts can share a direct invite link. Clicking the link joins you straight away; if you are signed out you are asked to sign in or register first and then brought back to finish joining. If the competition has already started you join as a spectator, and the host can make you a competitor.

### Scanning a QR Code (Mobile)

On the mobile app, tap the QR icon on the Join screen. Point your camera at the host's QR code. The invite code is read automatically and the join screen opens.

### Ghost Profiles

A host can create a **ghost profile** for someone who has not yet signed up. If you have been told a ghost profile exists for you:

1. Register a new account with your email
2. You will receive a claim link, or navigate to **Claim Profile** from the link the host shares
3. Confirm the claim

Once claimed, all results, medals, and stats from the ghost profile transfer permanently to your account. The ghost profile is removed.

## 3. Competing

### Understanding the Competition Structure

A competition contains a set of events chosen by the host. Each event is competed independently and results are submitted one at a time.

The host sets a **minimum events** number (N of M). Your total score is calculated from your best N results across all events you compete in. Doing more events than the minimum can only help you — only the best N count.

### Viewing the Events List

From inside a competition, go to the **Events** tab. Each event shows:
- Event name and category
- Status: Upcoming, Live, or Completed
- Your result (once submitted and confirmed)
- The event weight tag (affects how many points are awarded)

### Submitting a Result

An event must be marked **Live** by the host before you can submit. When an event is live:

1. Open the event from the Events tab
2. Tap or click **Submit Result**
3. Enter your result in the correct format (see below)
4. Optionally add an evidence link (to a photo or video)
5. Submit

**Result formats by event type:**

| Event type | Format | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Time (sprints, swimming) | `mm:ss.ms` or seconds | `11.40` or `1:04.32` |
| Distance (jumps, throws) | Metres with two decimal places | `6.85` |
| Score (football, basketball) | Whole number | `3` |
| Inverted score (golf) | Whole number — lower is better | `72` |
| Weight (weightlifting) | Kilograms | `100` |
| Possession (1v1 events) | Points won | `5` |

### Using the In-App Timer

For time-based events, a timer is available on the event screen. Tap **Start** when the event begins and **Stop** when it ends. The elapsed time is automatically entered as your result. You can still edit it manually before submitting.

### Understanding DNF

**DNF (Did Not Finish)** is applied by the host when a competitor starts an event but does not complete it — for example, a false start, an injury, or an incomplete round.

A DNF gives 0 points for that event and displays a red flag on your result. A DNF in a required event counts as one of your N minimum events.

Not submitting a result at all for an optional event carries no penalty.

### Attaching Evidence

When submitting a result you can tap **Add photo** to take or choose a photo (for example, the stopwatch, the scoreboard or a tape measure), or paste a link to a photo or video (YouTube, Google Drive, etc.). Photos are resized before upload. The host sees the evidence when confirming results.

Evidence helps the host confirm your result accurately. The host may request evidence before confirming.

### Raising a Dispute

After the host confirms a result you have **24 hours** to raise a dispute if you believe the result or place is incorrect.

1. Go to the Events tab and open the confirmed event
2. Tap **Raise Dispute**
3. Describe the issue

The host will review and either resolve or dismiss the dispute. You will be notified of the outcome.

## 4. Leaderboard and Scoring

### How Points Work

Points are awarded per event based on finishing place, then multiplied by the event's weight:

| Place | Base points |
|---|---|
| 1st | 10 |
| 2nd | 6 |
| 3rd | 3 |
| 4th | 1 |
| 5th | 0.5 |
| 6th and below (competed) | 0.1 |
| DNF / No-show | 0 |

Your total score is the sum of points from your best N events (where N is set by the host).

### Event Weighting

Each event has a weight tag that multiplies the base points:

| Tag | Multiplier |
|---|---|
| Not Important | 0.5× |
| Standard | 1.0× |
| Important | 1.5× |
| Very Important | 2.0× |
| Custom | 0.1× – 3.0× |

A **2× weight event** is worth twice as many points as a standard event. The weight tag is shown on each event in the Events tab.

### The Best-of-N Rule

The host sets a minimum number of events (N) each competitor must complete. Your total score uses only your best N results. If you compete in more events than the minimum, only the N highest-scoring events count toward your total. There is no penalty for entering more events.

### MVP Bonus and Worst Performer Penalty

For team events the host may enable MVP and worst performer voting:

- **MVP (+1 point):** After results are confirmed, competitors vote anonymously for the most valuable player among those who took part. The player with the most votes receives +1 point when the host finalises the awards (or when the competition is completed).
- **Worst performer (−1 point):** The same process in reverse. The player with the most votes receives −1 point.

Votes are anonymous. You can see your own vote but not how others voted.

### Tiebreakers

If two or more competitors are tied on total points at the end of the competition, the following sequence is used to break the tie:

1. Most gold medals
2. Most silver medals
3. Most bronze medals
4. **Sealed event nomination** — each tied competitor picks one event they both contested. Selections are hidden until both have submitted. The competitor who wins more of the two nominated events takes the higher place. If it is 1–1, the bigger raw winning margin decides.
5. **Host decides** — if all other steps are inconclusive

You will be notified if you are involved in a tiebreaker and need to nominate an event.

### The Leaderboard

The **Leaderboard** tab shows live standings updated in real time as results are confirmed. It displays total points, medal counts (gold, silver, bronze), and current rank for every competitor.

## 5. Social Features

### Activity Feed

The **Feed** tab inside a competition shows a chronological record of everything that has happened: results submitted, results confirmed, events started, MVP awards, and competition milestones. You can react to feed items with an emoji and leave comments.

### Group Chat

The **Chat** tab inside a competition is a group chat for all members. Messages appear in real time. You can use it to coordinate, trash-talk, or celebrate.

### Direct Messages

Go to **Messages** from the sidebar to send a direct message to any other user. Conversations are one-to-one and private.

### Reactions and Comments

On any activity feed item, tap the emoji icon to react or tap **Comment** to leave a reply. Reactions and comments are visible to all members.

### Reporting

If you see content that violates community standards — a competition name, a profile, a feed post or a message — use the **Report** button. Reports are reviewed by platform administrators.

### Blocking

To block someone, open their profile and tap **Block**. You will no longer see their chat messages or comments, neither of you can send the other a direct message, and you stop following them. They are not told. You can see and unblock the people you have blocked in **Profile settings → Blocked people**.

## 6. Profile and History

### Viewing Your Profile

Go to **Profile** from the sidebar to see your public profile. It shows your avatar, display name, country, bio, and career statistics.

### Career Stats

Your career stats are updated automatically after each confirmed result:

- Total competitions entered
- Total events competed in
- Gold, silver, and bronze medal counts
- Total points earned across all competitions

### Personal Bests

The **Personal Bests** section on your profile shows your best result for each event type you have competed in. Personal bests update automatically when a new confirmed result beats the previous best.

### Competition History

Your profile lists all competitions you have participated in, including your final rank and points for each.

### Viewing Other Profiles

Tap any competitor's name or avatar to view their public profile, where you can follow them, send a message, report or block them. Use **Find people** to search for other users by name.

### Deleting Your Account

Go to **Profile settings → Delete account** (on the web or in the app), type DELETE and confirm. This permanently removes your login, email address, profile details and photos, messages, comments, reactions, follows and personal bests, and you leave any competition that hasn't finished. Results you earned in finished competitions stay in those competitions under the name "Deleted user" so everyone else's standings stay correct. If you host a competition that hasn't finished, it is handed to your co-host or another competitor. Deletion cannot be undone.

## 7. Notifications

### What Triggers a Notification

You will receive a notification when:

- A result you submitted has been confirmed by the host
- A dispute you raised has been resolved
- MVP voting opens for an event you competed in
- You are named MVP or worst performer
- A tiebreaker involves you and requires your nomination
- The competition is completed and the podium is revealed
- Someone sends you a direct message or follows you
- A new season of one of your competitions starts
- You become the host of a competition

### Enabling Push Notifications (Mobile)

When you first open the mobile app you will be asked to allow notifications. Tap **Allow**. If you decline, you can re-enable them in your device's notification settings under **Social Olympics**.

Push notifications work when the app is in the background or fully closed. Tapping a notification opens the relevant screen directly.

### Web Notifications

On the web app, notifications appear in the **Notifications** tab in the sidebar and update in real time. Push notifications to your phone come from the mobile app.
